# Vocabulary Priority — Must / Useful / Hear-only (v2.4)

Guide for video + article tracks. It does NOT change the binding counts in
`references/track-video.md` / `references/track-article.md` — it tells you
**which words deserve those slots.**
Gold standard: Video01 (Entra Join).

> v2.4: adds the quality-upgrade rules — contextual examples, no orphan
> words, no cross-section repeats without a new learning purpose, CEFR-safe
> technical terms, natural Arabic glosses. Table shapes unchanged.

## The rule

> **Frequency alone does not determine educational value.**

Score every candidate word on five axes (1–3 each, pick the top scorers):

```text
Frequency + Meaning importance + IT relevance + Workplace usefulness + CEFR value
```

## Tiers (video)

- **Tier 1 — Must Know:** lesson fails without it. Required to understand the
  source. Fills the `**Must know**` table in `Key Words from the Video`.
  Example (Video01): `enrollment`, `credentials`, `hybrid`,
  `registration`, `automatic enrollment`, `source of authority`,
  `single sign-on`, `conditional access`, `group policy`, `device`,
  `configure`, `retire`, `wipe`, `sync`.
- **Tier 2 — Useful:** high work value, not critical for every point. Fills the
  `**Useful**` table + Collocations + Expansion.
  Example (Video01): `retire`, `wipe`, `sync`, `configure`, `migration`,
  `onboarding`, `pros and cons`, `by default`, `in reality`,
  `a couple of minutes`.
- **Tier 3 — Hear-only:** appeared but low study value. One line only:
  `You will also hear these - recognize them, do not study them: *...*`
  (Video01: `episode`, `stay tuned`, `Patreon`, `ring the bell`,
  `bomb that button`, `jibber-jabber`). Never a Key row.

## Mapping to the 11 video sections

- Key Words: Must table + Useful table, total 25–75 rows (validator).
- Phrases (8–13): transferable chunks from Tier 1/2
  (`by default`, `in reality`, `pros and cons`, `source of authority`).
- Sentences: Core authentic (understand source) + Practical steps +
  Reusable patterns (`Make sure that X is enabled.`) + Work (use tomorrow).
- Collocations (10–12): prefer `verb + IT noun`
  (`retire a device`, `wipe a device`, `sync a device`, `join a device`,
  `assign a license`, `configure hybrid join`, `update group policy`).
- Tier 3: zero Key slots, at most one passing mention each.

## Article variant (same tiers, different shape)

- Key Words from the Article: ONE table, 25–36 rows (no Must/Useful split).
- Essential English (`enforce`, `assign`, `require`, `allow`) + Technical IT
  English (`compliance policy`, `configuration profile`, `device enrollment`)
  mixed in that table.
- Advanced terminology (`remediation`, `provisioning`, `attestation`) → glosses
  in Expansion, B2 production only.

## v2.4 word-writing rules (all tracks)

- Every Key/Useful row: contextual example from the video's world (IT
  workplace preferred over generic). No orphan words — each row's example
  must reuse the word naturally.
- No repeat of the same headword across Key/Useful/Phrases/Collocations/
  Expansion unless the new section teaches a new use (new chunk, new pattern,
  new workplace transfer). Reuse inside the lesson is good; duplication as
  filler is not.
- Technical terms verbatim always (product names, cmdlets, error codes) —
  CEFR never simplifies them; difficulty grows via explanation length, not
  term substitution.
- Similar words get a 1-line distinction (`enrollment = management entry,
  registration = light personal entry`).
- Arabic: contextual meaning, not literal translation; B1/B2 taper per
  `language-system.md` (columns always present, no empty cells).
