## Day 002: Your IT Department
**Level:** A1 | **Speaking:** 30 seconds | **Output:** describe the department

### 1. Mission
By the end of this lesson, I can describe the structure of an IT department: what exists, which teams work there, and what each one does.

**Comprehension:** 1) How many teams? 2) What does the help desk do? 3) Who protects the systems?
### 8. Grammar in Context: there is / there are
> Goal: Learn the rule from lesson examples, then do the practice.

- **There is** a help desk in our office. (singular)
- **There are** two server rooms. (plural)
- **There is not** a security team. (negative)
- **Is there** a backup team? (question)

**Practice:**
1. Complete: "There ___ a network team. There ___ three managers."
2. Make a true sentence about your office.
3. Ask about a missing team: "___ there a [team]?"

### 9. Speaking (30 seconds)
> Goal: Read the task, study the model answer, then speak for 30 seconds.

**Task:** Describe your IT department: how many teams, who is responsible for what, and where the teams sit.

**Reference Answer (30 seconds):**
"Our IT department has five teams. The first team is the help desk; it answers the phone and closes simple tickets. The network team manages Wi-Fi and cables. The server team runs and checks the servers. The security team protects our systems. There is also a training team for new users. Our office is on floor two. Ask me about the teams any time."

### 11. Common Errors
> Goal: Avoid these 5 common mistakes.

1. ❌ There is five teams. ✅ There are five teams.
2. ❌ The network team is responsible of the network. ✅ The network team is responsible for the network.
3. ❌ The meeting is at 10 o'clock am. ✅ The meeting is at 10 a.m.
4. ❌ Where is located the server room? ✅ Where is the server room located?
5. ❌ We no have a backup team. ✅ We do not have a backup team.
