# Interview — REQ-Traced Questions, Due Diligence, Application Strategy, 30-60-90

All questions job-specific. Every question traces to its source requirement — a question without a REQ parent must not exist. Answers use only candidate evidence (`[E-xx]`) + bridge pattern for gaps.

## Question format (→ REPORT.md §8)

```text
Q01
Question: ...
Category: HR | Technical | Behavioral | Scenario | Candidate-specific
Source Requirement: REQ-007
Why likely: The job explicitly requires...
What interviewer tests: ...
Priority: P0
Answer structure: ...
Suggested answer (real evidence only, cite [E-xx] | bridge pattern for gaps)
Key points / Things to avoid
```

Cover: HR (intro, why role/company, why leaving, salary, start date, authorization) · Technical per major stack item (basic → advanced → troubleshooting → architecture → ops-real-world) · Behavioral STAR (conflict, failure, incident, prioritization, stakeholders, pressure, difficult user, delay) · Scenario (realistic to the role) · Candidate-specific (career jumps, cert without experience, employment gaps — only if explicit in profile). Plus a Study Guide table: Topic | Linked REQ(s) | What to Know | Practice Qs | Lab | Difficulty | Priority.

## Mock Interview (→ REPORT.md §9)

5 rounds: R1 HR ×10, R2 Technical ×15, R3 Scenario ×10, R4 Behavioral ×10, R5 Hiring Manager ×10. Per Q: Question / Source REQ / Expected Answer Elements / Candidate Answer Area (blank). Do not score unless user supplies answers.

## Cheat Sheet (→ REPORT.md §10)

60-sec intro, top achievements (with `[E-xx]`), strengths, one honest gap + mitigation, key tech, salary talking points (`[Sxxx]`), company facts, JD keywords — one page.

## Candidate Due Diligence (→ REPORT.md §8, closing block)

The interview goes both ways. Flag unclear or high-stakes JD items: reporting structure, team size, actual scope, on-call/rota, travel, work location, remote/hybrid status, technology ownership, budget responsibility, management responsibility, contract type, probation, salary transparency, benefits, required certifications, hands-on-vs-management ratio. One specific verification question per flagged item — never generic filler.

## 30-60-90 Plan (→ REPORT.md §11)

Grounded in JD responsibilities (cite REQs): 30d learn/relationships/quick wins, 60d assess/document/improve, 90d own/deliver — with learning, relationships, quick wins, tech assessment, docs, ops improvements, projects. Success metrics baselined in days 1–30, never pre-claimed.

## Application Strategy (→ REPORT.md §12)

Exactly one outcome, derived from documented gaps + gating + timing — never a hiring prediction: (1) Apply with current CV · (2) Tailor CV before applying · (3) Build evidence before applying · (4) Apply now while preparing · (5) Do not prioritize. Pair with Readiness: `READY NOW` / `READY WITH TARGETED PREPARATION` / `SIGNIFICANT PREPARATION REQUIRED` / `CURRENTLY MISALIGNED` (any gating GAP caps this at SIGNIFICANT PREPARATION REQUIRED or lower).
